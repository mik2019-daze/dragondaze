Isle of Wight Festivals

The three original Isle of Wight music festivals

 

1968

 

In 1968 Dave was studying at the Royal College of Art when the Foulk brothers asked him to design the first festival's poster and tickets.

 

1969

 

For the next two festivals in 1969 and 1970 Dave worked on site to deliver his increasingly complex designs.

 

1970

 

This was the big one, the largest musical event of its time with up to 700,000 fans and an amazing lineup including Jimi Hendrix and The who.
