Welcome to Dragondaze

the home of David Fairbother-Roe's artwork

Spectacular dragons, mythical beasts, iconic festival posters, intriguing pop-art experiments. Over his varied career Dave produced artwork for the original Isle of Wight Festivals, covers for Anne McCaffrey's pern books and album sleeves for Jon Anderson, Nazareth and Popol Ace.

Dragondaze presents for your enjoyment a range of Dave's work from the mid 1960s to the early 1980s. Astonishingly detailed fantasy landscapes next to surreal collages of Victorian engravings. Wonderfully colourful pop art designs beside iconic music posters.

Enter and explore the world of David Fairbrother-Roe. 

Why Dragondaze?

The Dragondaze Portfolio was a project of Dave's in the 1980s bringing together prints of the four paintings for Anne MacCaffrey’s Pern books in one package. Unfortunately it never saw the light of day.
